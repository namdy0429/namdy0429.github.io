## Prospective Students

### How to Get Involved

**Prospective PhD students:** Apply to UCI's PhD programs in Informatics or Software Engineering.

**Current UCI PhD students:** Interested in collaboration? Email me - let's talk!

**UCI undergraduate and Master's students:** The best way to express your interest is to fill out [this form](https://forms.gle/jnBjKFAQshYTBMi48). We check submissions regularly and will reach out if we find a good fit.

### A Note on Email
You're welcome to email with questions, but I may not be able to reply to every message. [This FAQ by Yonatan Bisk](https://talkingtorobots.com/FAQ.html) provides helpful context about faculty email practices.
